# 🎬 Netflix Data Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project analyzing Netflix Movies and TV Shows using Microsoft Power BI. This project explores content distribution, release trends, genres, ratings, and geographical patterns to transform raw data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze Netflix's content catalog and understand its composition, distribution, and trends over time.

Using Power BI, the project transforms Netflix data into interactive visualizations that help explore content patterns and support data-driven recommendations.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Understand the distribution of Movies and TV Shows.
- Analyze content trends across release years.
- Identify the most common genres and categories.
- Explore content distribution across countries.
- Examine ratings and content classifications.
- Extract actionable insights to support content strategy decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Microsoft Power BI | Dashboard development and data visualization |
| Power Query | Data cleaning and transformation |
| DAX | Calculated measures and analytical calculations |
| Data Modeling | Organizing data for analysis |
| Data Visualization | Presenting insights through interactive charts |

## 🔍 Analysis & Key Areas

### 1. Content Distribution
- Movies vs. TV Shows distribution.
- Comparison of content types.
- Overview of Netflix's content library.

### 2. Content Trends
- Content distribution by release year.
- Historical patterns in Netflix's catalog.
- Changes in content availability over time.

### 3. Genre Analysis
- Most common genres and categories.
- Distribution of content across different genres.
- Exploration of content variety.

### 4. Geographical Analysis
- Content distribution by country.
- Comparison of content production across regions.
- Identification of geographical patterns.

### 5. Ratings Analysis
- Distribution of content ratings.
- Comparison of content classifications.
- Understanding audience suitability categories.

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis focuses on identifying meaningful patterns within Netflix's content catalog, including:

- The balance between Movies and TV Shows.
- The distribution of content across different genres.
- The geographical concentration of Netflix titles.
- Content release patterns across different years.
- The distribution of audience content ratings.

These findings provide a clearer understanding of Netflix's content library and its characteristics.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical areas explored in this project, the following recommendations can support content strategy decisions:

1. **Content Diversification:** Explore opportunities to expand underrepresented genres and content categories.
2. **Regional Strategy:** Use geographical distribution patterns to identify potential opportunities in different markets.
3. **Content Planning:** Monitor historical release trends to support future content planning.
4. **Audience Segmentation:** Use content ratings and classifications to better understand different audience segments.
5. **Data-Driven Decisions:** Continuously monitor content distribution and trends to support strategic planning.
